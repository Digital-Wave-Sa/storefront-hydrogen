feat(cake): catalog-driven options, prices from Shopify

Splits identity from price.

catalog.ts holds identity — 23 formats with their centimetre dimensions, 10
fillings, 22 coating colours, 16 decorations — generated from the vendor's
catalog.json with English labels added, since their package is Arabic-only. It
holds no prices at all.

Prices come from the cake_attribute metaobject, which the store already used,
joined by a new builder_key field. Deliberately not joined on name_english: that
is free text an admin will reasonably fix a typo in one day, silently unpricing
whatever it was attached to. A key the client never sees is a key the client
never breaks.

Three states, and the distinctions are load-bearing:

  unpriced     no row at all. No total can be shown. Blocks checkout.
  provisional  a price we modelled. Full total shown. Notice displayed.
  priced       the client's number. Sells.

A price_delta of 0 means free and is a valid answer; an absent row is not. That
is why the seeding must never fill blanks with placeholder numbers — a row with
a number in it is indistinguishable from one the client filled in.

cake-pricing.ts is the arithmetic, with no numbers in it, returning a line
breakdown plus a missing[] array. Extras are flat: a filling adds the same to a
15 cm cake as to a 50 cm one. Changing that is a change to both the function and
the price book shape, and it is the expensive one.

docs/cake-pricing-setup.graphql documents the metaobject setup, including the
DRAFT trap: cake_attribute has the publishable capability, so a metaobject
created without an explicit ACTIVE status is invisible to the Storefront API.
It reads as "everything is free" rather than "nothing is priced", because a
DRAFT row and a missing row look identical from the storefront while a zero and
a missing row do not.
