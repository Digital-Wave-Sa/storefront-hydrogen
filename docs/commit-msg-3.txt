feat(cake): render the builder with the V5 engine

Swaps CakePreview for CakeRenderer inside CustomCakeBuilder, and drives every
option list from the catalog instead of from metaobject display names.

The engine is imported dynamically inside an effect. It reaches for document and
createImageBitmap at module scope, so a static import would pull that into the
server bundle and break SSR for the whole route. Renders are serialised by a
revision ticket and debounced a frame: a compose is hundreds of milliseconds of
synchronous work, and a slider drag fires far faster than that.

canvasId="cake-3d-canvas" is load-bearing. handleCheckout captures the order's
preview with getElementById on that exact id. Drop it and the order still
completes, just without a picture of the cake — a failure nobody notices until a
baker phones.

Deleted rather than ported:

  ~60 lines of cake_topping_design filtering. Each topping used to be a
  photograph taken on one shape, so offering it for another put the wrong
  artwork on the preview. decorationInstances walks the real footprint and
  places sprites along it, so there is no per-shape artwork left to mismatch.

  supportedViews, which tested for an imageTop/imageSliced file and fell back
  to a hardcoded list of five shape ids. The renderer draws any view of any
  format from one mesh.

  Eleven hand-picked frosting hexes. The old preview recoloured by scanning
  pixels, so any hex would do; the new one multiplies a luminance curve of
  photographed white fondant by the colour the client sampled off their own
  printed swatch, so an invented hex renders a coating the bakery cannot make.

Two latent bugs fixed while in here:

  photoPrintPrice searched every metaobject for a name containing "photo",
  "print", "upload" or "image". A decoration called "Printed Ribbon" would have
  matched and charged its price for uploading a picture. Now one exact key.

  The saved-design restore called setCurrentStep(4) unconditionally, on the
  assumption a restore had rebuilt a whole cake. Unmatched values stay null by
  design, so it could land the customer on the writing step looking at a
  placeholder with nothing selected. The catalog swap turned that from an edge
  case into the normal path — saved designs name a shape "ستاندرد صغير" and the
  new formats are addressed round-20x20-h8, so nothing from before can match.
  It now lands on the first unanswered step and says the design could not be
  restored.

Heart is gone: the vendor's contour() draws circles and rounded rectangles only.
That removes two products the client may be selling — قلب صغير and قلب.
