feat(cake): vendor the Saadeddin V5 cake renderer

The vendor shipped a complete, unminified procedural renderer — a software
rasterizer that builds a triangle mesh in centimetres, projects it
orthographically, and samples photographic fondant and filling textures per
pixel with a z-buffer. It replaces an approach based on flat per-shape
photographs, which is why a square cake previously had no top view: nobody had
photographed one.

render-core.ts and finishing.ts are copied byte-for-byte. The only edits are a
header and the sibling import path. The projection maths, the UV atlas offsets
and the four-camera table are tuned against their photographic masters, so
tidying them shows up as a cake that no longer lines up with its own texture.
Anything we want to change belongs in compose.ts, which wraps them — that keeps
their next drop a straight overwrite.

91 WebP assets under public/cake/v5, with the vendor's content hashes stripped
from the filenames since Oxygen handles cache-busting for /public itself. All 69
catalog paths and all 22 runtime textures verified present before commit.

/cake-render-test is a diagnostic route carrying a live millisecond readout. It
exists to answer one question — whether ~880,000 pixels shaded in JavaScript per
frame is fast enough on a mid-range phone, or whether paintGeometry needs moving
to WebGL. Delete it before launch.
